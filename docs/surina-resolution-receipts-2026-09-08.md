# Surina resolution receipts

This player-experience slice adds a concise result card for five Surina workflows without changing their engine rules:

1. Attack rolls and damage
2. Movement and movement interruptions
3. Breath Weapon
4. Lay on Hands
5. Required defensive responses

Each receipt keeps the engine summary and separately lists the concrete state changes a player needs to track: hit-point changes, conditions gained or removed, start and end map coordinates, movement spent, resource-pool changes, Reaction use, Action use, and any required follow-up response. The card appears directly beneath the persistent turn guide and can be dismissed.

The receipt derives its facts from the encounter state before and after the verified engine operation. It does not reimplement legality, targeting, dice, damage, conditions, or resource rules in the interface. Registry coverage remains 95 fully supported, 3 partial, and 1 descriptive.
