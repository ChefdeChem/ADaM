# Surina playability milestone: five mechanic interaction pass

This slice improves exactly five registry mechanics in Surina's verified combat profile without changing her imported 2014 build or inventing 2024 character grants.

1. Fire Resistance now has a visible persistent defense indicator and names the rolled and applied damage in the combat log.
2. Breath Weapon now requires an explicit legal aim, previews every affected creature for both the 15-foot Cone and 30-foot Line, and warns through the preview when allies are included.
3. Divine Sense now reports a live snapshot throughout its active duration, so creature locations and consecrated or desecrated presence update as the encounter changes.
4. Lay on Hands now has its own touch-target step. A previously selected attack target is never silently reused as the healing target.
5. Unarmed Strike now presents Damage, Grapple, and Shove as one clear three-option rules surface.

The character source remains detected as 2014. Combat resolution remains 2024. Source uses and resource recovery stay unchanged, so Divine Sense and Breath Weapon do not receive grants that belong only to a converted character.

Registry coverage remains 95 fully supported, 3 partial, and 1 descriptive. These are interaction and usability completions for mechanics already counted as supported, not five newly supported registry entries.

Surina is ready for a player-operated end-to-end test of the bounded trainer loop: choose held equipment, roll initiative, move, attack, use all three Unarmed Strike options, use Breath Weapon, Divine Sense, and Lay on Hands, resolve reactions and conditions, finish combat, rest, and start another encounter. This milestone does not claim simulation of every possible tabletop object, narrative ruling, lighting condition, or custom Ready trigger.

Rules basis: official SRD 5.2.1 pages 17, 53 to 55, 84, and 190. Checked September 8, 2026.
