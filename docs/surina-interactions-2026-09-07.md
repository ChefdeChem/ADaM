# Surina interaction follow-up

This release improves four previously blocked core workflows. It does not change the 99-entry character registry totals (85 supported, 13 partial, one descriptive) or convert Surina's 2014 build.

- **Help:** distract an enemy within 5 feet for the next ally attack against that enemy, with expiry at the helper's next turn; stabilize an adjacent unconscious ally with a DC 10 Medicine check; assist a proficient skill after the player confirms that the adjacent ally can understand and use that assistance. Skill Help applies and is consumed only by the matching next check. Solo encounters correctly have no eligible Help recipient. Surina's four skill proficiencies are detected from the verified skill modifiers matching ability plus proficiency; source records remain unchanged. Tool assistance and arbitrary remote assistance remain outside this surface.
- **Hide:** current maps support Total Cover using walls. Half cover is rejected. A DC 15 Stealth check includes Chain Mail disadvantage and stores the resulting Perception DC. Invisible is removed after an attack roll, explicit loud speech, a modeled noisy door, or successful enemy Search. Enemy Search spends its Action and cannot also attack. The scripted map uses a bounded search abstraction, not a complete sound/special-sense simulation. Heavy obscurement, three-quarters cover and spell-component integration remain incomplete. Casting while hidden is explicitly blocked until component metadata is verified; Surina has no source-build spells. The player may explicitly end Hide before casting.
- **Ready:** choose a carried weapon and enemy. The supported trigger is that enemy finishing movement, including movement resumed after an opportunity-attack choice. The Action is spent immediately; release remains optional, requires a Reaction, sight and legal range, and uses separate player attack/damage clicks. An ignored trigger does not spend the Reaction. The enemy's Action and turn survive the response. The preparation expires before the helper's next turn and cannot release while incapacitated. Other triggers, readied movement and readied spells remain unimplemented.
- **Objects:** an optional, clearly named squeaky door practice setup provides a real map interaction. Opening/closing updates wall obstruction. The first simple interaction is free; another requires Utilize. Locked or occupied doorways are rejected. The door's noise is explicit scenario metadata, not a universal rule about all doors. Undefined equipment, unlocking methods and object-specific DCs are not invented.

Authority: official SRD 5.2.1, interacting with objects p. 13, stabilizing p. 18, Help and Hide pp. 182-183, Ready pp. 186-187. Verified September 7, 2026: https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf

Follow-up: the approved plain-view Hide ruling and held-weapon support are documented in `surina-hide-and-hands-2026-09-07.md`. Quiet doors now also end Hide when opening them exposes the creature to ordinary enemy observation. Magical invisibility remains separate.

Twelve focused interaction regressions cover costs, invalid choices, expiry, attack-target matching, armor disadvantage, enemy discovery, resumed movement, separate reaction rolls and enemy continuation. Run the complete test, lint, typecheck and production build gate before release. No browser-operated QA is claimed.

Future 2014 and 2024 encounter-resolution settings remain separate from source edition detection and are not implemented by this release.
