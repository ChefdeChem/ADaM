# Registry and import completion slice

## Five bounded mechanics

1. Cleira's Charm Person now accepts any visible humanoid, applies the Charmed restriction, records the friendly relationship, grants Advantage on social checks against that target, ends after allied harm, and reveals the caster when it ends.
2. Pharos's Charm Person uses the same complete resolution while preserving the spell's 2014 source provenance.
3. Druidcraft now requires and records the scenario or DM weather outlook and player-authored harmless sensory detail before map placement. Its bloom and small-flame choices remain executable.
4. Flattened D&D Beyond imports now record a source-edition assessment from explicit feature clues. Ambiguous and mixed sheets remain labeled for review rather than being silently converted.
5. Imported records without parsed attacks remain unchanged. The trainer derives a current-resolution Unarmed Strike only in the playable profile so the character can enter a bounded encounter without overwriting the upload.

## Coverage

The 99-entry registry now reports 98 fully supported, 0 partial, and 1 descriptive entry. Control Flames remains descriptive because its user-imported sheet does not contain a lawful executable option definition and the spell is not available in the permitted SRD sources.

## Authorities and boundaries

- SRD 5.1, Charm Person and the Charmed condition.
- SRD 5.1, Druidcraft.
- SRD 5.2.1 remains the default combat-resolution authority for derived playable profiles.
- Source records and edition evidence remain separate from derived combat profiles.
