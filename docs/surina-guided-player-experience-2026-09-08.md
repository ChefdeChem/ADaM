# Surina guided player experience

This slice turns Surina's verified combat loop into the reference experience for future characters. It does not add or reclassify registry mechanics.

## Player flow

- A persistent four-stage guide identifies the current step: Start, Choose, Resolve, or Finish.
- The guide prioritizes mandatory responses over new actions and directs the player to the relevant control.
- Attack and spell selection move focus to the tactical map. Target selection moves focus back to the required roll.
- Once Surina spends her Action, the guide explains remaining movement and provides a direct End Turn control.
- Encounter setup collapses after the encounter is built or initiative is rolled, keeping the active decision close to the top of the play surface.
- Surina's HP, AC, remaining movement, Action, and Reaction remain visible inside the guide.

## Scope and honesty

Surina remains the reference character for the bounded scripted combat and recovery loop. This interface guides supported choices without claiming that every free-form tabletop declaration has an automated resolution. Her detected 2014 character build remains intact, while verified 2024 combat resolution and player-facing edition notes continue to apply under the project's edition policy.

Registry coverage remains 95 fully supported, 3 partial, and 1 descriptive. The next player test should evaluate whether a beginner can build an encounter, roll initiative, move, choose and resolve actions, respond to enemies, finish combat, and recover without relying on the raw combat log.
