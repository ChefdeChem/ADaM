# Surina primary action surface

This interface slice makes five high-frequency Surina workflows easier to understand without changing their registered rules or engine resolution:

1. Attack
2. Movement
3. Breath Weapon
4. Lay on Hands
5. Dodge

The primary-action cards show whether each workflow is available, whether it starts a guided sequence, its action-economy cost, and its current resource balance when applicable. A blocked card displays the engine's existing legality reason rather than a second UI-only rule interpretation.

Attack remains a multi-step workflow even when no target is selected. Choosing it opens the existing weapon and Unarmed Strike choices, followed by legal-target highlighting and separate attack and damage rolls. Movement moves focus directly to the tactical map. Breath Weapon and Lay on Hands continue into their tested shape, aim, target, and resource panels. Dodge continues to resolve through the existing effect engine.

The full action categories remain available beneath the primary surface for tactical, skill, object, and less common choices. No character data, feature ownership, rules provenance, or registry support label changes in this slice. Coverage remains 95 fully supported, 3 partial, and 1 descriptive.
