# Surina complete trainer playthrough milestone

Surina's detected 2014 level-one Dragonborn Paladin build remains unchanged. Her imported features, resources, equipment, choices, and source provenance are preserved. Encounter resolution continues to default to verified 2024 rules, with player-facing notes where the source and applied behavior differ.

## Milestone reached

Surina is fully playable through the trainer's supported combat-and-recovery loop:

- choose held equipment, roll initiative, move with terrain and opportunity attacks, split movement around actions, and use Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Stand Up, and End Turn;
- make Glaive, Longsword, two-handed Longsword, Javelin, thrown Javelin, and Unarmed Strike attacks with legal targeting, range, hands, ammunition, attack rolls, damage, critical hits, and defeat handling;
- use current-resolution Shove and Grapple, including the target's chosen Strength or Dexterity save, a free-hand requirement, Speed 0, attack Disadvantage against creatures other than the grappler, Athletics or Acrobatics escape, enemy escape behavior, dragging costs, hazards, voluntary release, and automatic release on incapacitation or separation;
- use Breath Weapon, Divine Sense, Lay on Hands, and Fire Resistance with source resources and current-resolution behavior;
- resolve targeting, saving throws, reactions, concentration, conditions, hazards, unconsciousness, death saves, victory, Short Rest Hit Die healing, Long Rest recovery, and state carried into a new encounter;
- use Search, Study, Influence, Help, Total-Cover Hide, modeled doors, and two deterministic Ready triggers: after selected-enemy movement and when the enemy first becomes a legal target for the selected weapon.

The derived playable profile distinguishes the imported Glaive mastery label from a feature actually owned by the character. It applies no Graze rider, grants no Weapon Mastery, and leaves the imported source record untouched. All 17 mechanics and equipment modes exposed by Surina's derived trainer profile report fully supported. The project-wide source registry remains 85 supported, 13 partial, and 1 descriptive because core interaction work and a derived weapon mode do not rewrite or inflate the 99 source entries.

## Deliberate boundaries

This milestone means a complete executable Surina playthrough within the trainer, not comprehensive support for every tabletop circumstance. Custom Ready wording, undefined objects, narrative skill outcomes, lighting and special-sense simulation, interrupted adventure rests, player-versus-player grappling, Reaction Shoves, and class alternatives Surina does not own remain outside this bounded surface or require adjudication. Current maps explicitly assume visible lighting.

Separate selectable 2014 and 2024 encounter-resolution engines remain a roadmap item. Character source edition stays independent of encounter resolution, and neither character conversion nor an unimplemented setting is claimed here.

## Rules basis

- [Official SRD 5.2.1](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf), checked September 8, 2026: Ready, Grappled, Unarmed Strike, movement, conditions, reactions, rests, and existing current-resolution features.
- [Official 2014 Basic Rules: Combat](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014/combat), checked September 8, 2026: source-edition Grapple and Shove contrast.

No proprietary rule text, converted character choices, or new character grants were added.
